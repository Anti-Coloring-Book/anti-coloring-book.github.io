Privacy Policy: The Anti-Coloring Book app

Effective October 10, 2026

The Anti-Coloring Book app ("the app") is published by Susan Striker (Young at Art). It is a drawing app for children. This policy explains what information the app handles, and why. In short: no ads, no tracking, no accounts, and your child's drawings stay on the device.

What we do not collect

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other contact details.
  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No location, contacts, camera, or microphone.
  • No advertising, and no advertising or marketing identifiers.
  • No analytics or behavior tracking.
  • No chat, social features, or public sharing inside the app.

Drawings

Drawings are saved only on your device. They are never uploaded to us. A drawing leaves the device only if you choose to save it to your photo library, print it, or share it using your device's own share menu. On iPhone and iPad the app can only add pictures to your photo library; it cannot see your photos.

Information the app does use

InformationWhyWho handles it
A random app ID, created on first launch. It is not linked to any name or email. To remember which books were bought on this device, and to restore them. Google Firebase (anonymous sign-in)
Purchase records: which book was bought, and when. To unlock books you paid for and allow "Restore Purchases". Payments are handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see card details. Apple App Store or Google Play, and RevenueCat (purchase records)
A device check that confirms the app is genuine. To protect the book pages from being copied by unofficial apps. Apple App Attest or Google Play Integrity, through Firebase App Check
Crash reports: what went wrong, the device model, system version, app version, and a random installation ID. To find and fix crashes. Google Firebase Crashlytics
Standard server request logs: the time of each request, which book's pages were requested, and the IP address of the device. They are deleted after 30 days. To deliver book pages and to find and fix problems. Google Cloud (Firebase)

We do not sell any information, and we do not share it with anyone except the providers listed above, who handle it only to run the app.

Read-aloud and book links

The "Listen" button reads a page's prompt aloud using your device's built-in voice. Some pages suggest a children's book to read. The links for those books open the Amazon or Barnes & Noble website in your browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Children's privacy

The app is made for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app does not ask for any. Purchases go through the parent-controlled App Store or Google Play purchase screens.

Security

Book pages are downloaded over encrypted connections and stored encrypted on the device.

Delete your data

This applies to Anti-Coloring Book Collection by Susan Strikers Young at Art® (the developer name on Google Play). There is no account to close.

  1. On the device: uninstall the app. This deletes all drawings, downloaded book pages and the app's random ID from that device right away.
  2. On our side: email susanstriker24.apps@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Anti-Coloring Book data". If you bought a book, include the order number from your Google Play or Apple receipt (for example GPA.1234-5678-9012-34567), so we can find the records that belong to you. We never ask for anything else.

Within 30 days of your request we delete:

  • the random app ID and its link to your purchases (Firebase and RevenueCat).

Deleted automatically, without a request:

  • server request logs, after 30 days;
  • crash reports, after 90 days.

Kept: the purchase itself, which Apple or Google hold under their own policies. That is what lets you restore a book you bought, even after we delete our records.
